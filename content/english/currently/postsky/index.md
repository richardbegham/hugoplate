The first year was primarily focused as a much-needed extended period to decompress and focus on personal and family matters, together with starting on home improvements.  

There was still time for sone work-related matters : After many years of being too busy and being constantly rescheduled, Richard was finally able to rejoin the [Committee of the STLD](http://www.stld.org.uk/) - which he had previously served on for 10 years, including being Secretary for some of that time.

This led to an interesting trip to see the BBC Belfast Studios - as well as the city for the first time.
And also the privilege to be a full delegate at the amazing and inspirational [Showlight 2025 in Dijon](http://www.showlight.org/) for the first time since 2013.
