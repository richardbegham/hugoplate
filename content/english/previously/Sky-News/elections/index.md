Richard designed, built, and ran these for the UK in 2015, 2017, 2019, and for the USA in 2020.

The most spectacular studio was the 2019 UK General Election, where large parts of the huge Sky Central office build were taken-over.

{{< tab "2015 UK" >}}

#### 2015 UK General Election (Studio C)

Studio C, adjacent to the then-main Studio News Studio A, had previously only been used as a Green-Screen studio during the set refurbishment in 2010, and as a small situation room during the MH370 mystery. Apart from that, its only other appearance on-air was as an Election Night Speical room in 2015. Presented by Adam Boulton surrounded by a room full of analysists and large monitors. At the same time, at the 5m high videowall (lit by Richard back in 2013) out in the main studio, Ed Conway and the Graphics team got very excitedto stretch their legs... and arms...

#### Exterior Lighting

Just 4 days beforehand, Richard was asked if he could jazz-up the exterior of the site somehow, here's a large pot of money, a helicopter, and let us know if you need all the flights into Heathrow diverted... Ouch! Thanks to a top crew from ELP, and the CAA being unusually cooperative, 48x Mythos II were used to make a giant X in the sky. The best that could be obtained at short-notice.

The colours used are the colours of the 7 main parties - for example, Gold is SNP and Yellow is Lib Dem - as total balance must always be shown on-air.

And, yes, he did have all the flights into Heathrow diverted - to the North Runway...

#### Giant Letters (The Hub)

The Sky Hub building - standing-in visually as Sky News's HQ in order to reference the debate a few weeks earlier, was lit up, although a bit more modestly than the year before. Custom gobos were used, one per letter, to project the words In or Out? flashing alternatively. This was used for the grand reveal of the result when the flashing stopped. SurperSharpies were used this time round.
