## Introduction

Richard Bowles has worked in the Entertainment/Media Industry since the early 1990s.

He has specialised in Lighting for Multi-Camera Live Broadcasting for over a quarter of a century.

For 15 years until recently, he working for Sky Television (UK) as a Lighting Director. Whilst there he was responsible for the studio lighting for Sky News (UK), Sky Sports, Sky Arts, Sky Cinema, Sky One, and others. He also designed, built, and supported studios fully equipped with LED luminaries and innovative remote control systems.
