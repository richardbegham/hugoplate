## Gathering of Personal Information

This site is designed to be a "data desert." We don't want your data, and we certainly don't sell it.

- **Cookies:** Not a single cookie is hoarded here. You won't find any "Accept" banners because there is nothing to accept.
- **Analytics:** We don't track your movements or profile your behaviour.
- **Third Parties:** We do not use third-party tracking scripts that phone home to big tech.

However, as this site is hosted by Cloudflare, they may insert some cookies for their own purposes, which we have no control over.

## If You Get In Touch

If you explicitly ask us to get in touch via email, we will process your name and email address solely to respond to your inquiry.

- **Legal Basis:** We process this under **Legitimate Interests** (answering your questions).
- **Retention:** We only keep your contact details as long as necessary to complete our conversation.
- **Your Rights:** Under the UK GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, or erase any personal data we hold about you. Contact Richard Bowles at GDPR@LightingDir.Com to exercise these rights.
