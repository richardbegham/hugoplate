## Richard's lights

When working in everywhere from world-class studios to semi-derelict barns, there's often a whole range of lighting equipment available to use, either in-house or hired-in. The right light for one situation is not necessarily the right light for another.

However, for Budget, Efficacy, or size reasons; sometimes having to hand your own light-weight but powerful equipment is the most appropriate solution.

### Litepanels Astra X6

- This 1x1 format panel light gives an incredible combiantion of light output and light weight gives 1583 Lux at 3m, with fully variable colour temperature from 3200 to 5600K, all flicker-free and with a remarkable (and genuine) CRI/TCLI between 97 & 98.
- And all that whilst weighing-in at just a smidge over 3kg; making this a rare lights that you can safely rig one-handed! {{< sidenote >}} A memorable aspect of the original Vari*Lite VL-5 specification{{< /sidenote >}}

### Wireless Uplighters

- Fantastically flexible, versatile, and stay on for ages.
- These units have improved on the original Smartbat design with 6 colour emmitters instead of 3 (giving a wider colour gamut as well as increased output), as well as longer-lasting batteries and the more robust Powercon connectors instead of IEC.
- Extra sets of these is very mildly addictive! 🤣

### 2-Cell Blinders

- LED version of the classic 2-cell Molephay, except without the miles of socapex, dimmers, and heat.
